Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

ClearStatus is an iOS app that converts videos from your photo library into clips optimized for sharing on social media apps, such as WhatsApp Status. This policy explains what information ClearStatus handles and how.

ClearStatus collects no data. Everything happens on your device.

What ClearStatus does with your videos

  • Videos you select are read from your photo library and processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party by the app.
  • Converted clips are saved back to your photo library or shared only when you explicitly choose to do so, using Apple's standard share sheet.
  • Temporary working files created during conversion are stored in the app's private on-device storage and are cleaned up automatically.

Data collection

ClearStatus does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports
  • Your videos, photos, or any media content

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not use tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

Photo library access

ClearStatus requests photo library access solely to let you pick videos for conversion and to save converted clips back to your library. Access is governed by iOS permissions that you can change at any time in Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

ClearStatus Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment process: apple.com/legal/privacy

Children's privacy

ClearStatus does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
