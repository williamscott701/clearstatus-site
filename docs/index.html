Why ClearStatus

Built to survive the WhatsApp upload

Every part of the pipeline is tuned around one goal: what you post to WhatsApp Status should look like what you shot.

Full-HD, not mush

Videos are sized to upload at the highest quality WhatsApp will actually keep — no forced second-round compression.

Auto-split long clips

Long videos are automatically cut to fit WhatsApp Status's 90-second clip limit — pick 30, 60, or 90 seconds and choose exactly which parts to post.

Right side up, every time

Orientation and the full frame are preserved — no cropping, no upside-down clips, and HDR footage tone-maps correctly so colors don't wash out.

100% on-device

Fast, no waiting rooms

Privacy & Security

Your videos never leave your phone

ClearStatus is built to be private by default — not as an afterthought. Here's exactly what does and doesn't leave your phone.

  • All video processing happens entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Advertisers never see your videos. Nothing about the clip you're converting is shared with Google or anyone else.
  • Ads are non-personalized. The free version's optional rewarded ads don't profile you, and the app never asks to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Premium is fully ad-free — for subscribers the ad SDK is never even started.
  • No account required to use the app — nothing to sign up for, nothing tied to your identity.
  • Subscriptions are billed entirely through Apple — we never see or store your payment details.
